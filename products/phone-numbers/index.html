FAQ
Questions teams ask first.
What kinds of numbers can I get?+
Local, national and toll-free numbers. Search by city or type in the dashboard or through the Numbers API.
Do I need KYC?+
Yes. Business KYC is completed online before numbers go live, as India's telecom rules require.
Can I move a number between agents?+
Yes. Re-point a number to a different agent, SIP trunk or call flow at any time. A number can be bound to one agent at a time.
Can I release a number?+
Yes. Release numbers you no longer need from the dashboard or the API.
