FAQ
Questions teams ask first.
Something else? Talk to the team.
What kinds of phone numbers can I buy in India?+
Local, national and toll-free numbers. Search by city or type in the dashboard or through the Numbers API.
Do I need KYC to buy an Indian virtual number?+
Yes. India's telecom rules require business verification. TryVox runs business KYC online before numbers go live.
Can I use the same number for my AI agent and my team?+
A number binds to one AI agent at a time. You can re-point it to a SIP trunk, a call flow or a different agent whenever you need.
How fast can I get a number live?+
Most teams search, verify and route a number in minutes once business KYC is complete.
Is TryVox an alternative to Exotel or Plivo for phone numbers?+
Yes, for teams running AI voice agents. TryVox gives you Indian numbers, SIP, a Voice API and agent bindings in one account, priced per minute.
