FAQ

Questions teams ask first.

Something else? Talk to the team.

Why does TryVox need business KYC?+

India's telecom rules require business verification before numbers are issued.

What is DLT registration?+

Distributed Ledger Technology registration, required in India for commercial SMS. TryVox links your DLT entity and templates.

How do verified caller IDs work?+

You verify that you own a number, then use it as the caller ID on outbound calls.

Can I record consent for the people I call?+

Yes. Store consent per contact and respect withdrawals.
