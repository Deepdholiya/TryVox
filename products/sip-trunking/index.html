FAQ

Questions teams ask first.

What is a SIP trunk?+

A SIP trunk carries phone calls over the internet between the phone network and your systems, such as a voice agent or PBX.

Can I restrict who uses my trunk?+

Yes. Use SIP credentials, IP access lists, or both.

How many calls can a trunk carry?+

You set a channel limit per trunk. Talk to us if you need more capacity.

Can I use my own carrier?+

Yes. Bring Your Own Carrier (BYOC) keeps your carrier while using TryVox on top.
