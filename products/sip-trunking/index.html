FAQ

Questions teams ask first.

Something else? Talk to the team.

What is SIP trunking?+

A SIP trunk carries phone calls over the internet between the public phone network and your systems, such as a voice agent, LiveKit or a PBX.

Can I use TryVox SIP trunks with LiveKit?+

Yes. Point LiveKit or any standards-based SIP client at your TryVox trunk domain with your credentials.

How do I stop unauthorised use of my trunk?+

Use SIP credentials, IP access lists, or both. Rotate credentials any time.

How many concurrent calls can a trunk carry?+

You set a channel limit per trunk. Talk to us if you need more capacity.

Can I bring my own carrier?+

Yes. Bring Your Own Carrier keeps your routes and rates while you use TryVox routing and call events on top.
