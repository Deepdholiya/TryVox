FAQ
Questions teams ask first.

      

        
What is TryVox?+
TryVox is telephony infrastructure for voice AI. We provide phone numbers, SIP trunking, programmable voice APIs, call routing and real-time call events that connect any AI voice agent to the phone network.

        
Do you provide an AI voice agent?+
No. You bring your own agent, built on Vapi, Retell, LiveKit, Bolna, ElevenLabs or your own code, and we connect it to real phone calls.

        
Can I get Indian phone numbers for my AI agent?+
Yes. Local, national and toll-free numbers, with business KYC and India's telecom requirements handled during setup.

        
How do I connect TryVox to Vapi or Retell?+
Create a SIP trunk in the console, copy the credentials into your agent platform and assign a number. Then place a test call.

        
Is TryVox only for voice AI?+
No. The same account covers SIP, programmable voice, WhatsApp, SMS and alerts.

        
How is TryVox priced?+
Pay as you go, with no lock-in. Use the estimator above and ask for a rate card for your volume.
